FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I make a reservation?

Call us (+90 352 207 00 00), message us on WhatsApp (+90 506 752 02 86), use the form on this page or email info@cityonekayseri.com. Just send your dates and number of guests and we will share availability and rates right away.

What are the breakfast hours?

Our rich open buffet breakfast is served every morning from 07:00 to 10:00.

Are the spa and pool free for guests?

Yes. Guests enjoy the semi-Olympic pool, jacuzzi, Turkish hammam, Finnish hammam, sauna and fitness areas free of charge. The spa is open 09:00 – 23:00 on weekdays and 09:00 – 21:00 on weekends.

Is Wi-Fi free?

Yes, we offer free Wi-Fi throughout the hotel.

Is room service available?

Yes, room service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Is there parking or valet service?

Valet service is available at the entrance.

Can I book a hall for a wedding or a meeting?

Of course. With six event halls and Sini Restaurant (up to 750 guests) we host weddings, engagements, henna nights, meetings and corporate events. Send your date and guest count and we will prepare a tailored offer.
