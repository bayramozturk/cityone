KVKK Privacy Notice

Data controller: Panorama Mobilya İnşaat Turizm Otel Sanayi Ticaret A.Ş. – City One Hotel & Spa. The official, legally binding text of the notice is the Turkish version; this page is an English summary for convenience.

In line with Article 10 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”) and the related communiqué, we inform you how your personal data is processed.

1. Data controller

City One Hotel & Spa, Eski Sanayi Bölgesi 2. Cadde No: 11, Kayseri / Türkiye. Phone: +90 352 207 00 00, email: info@cityonekayseri.com.

2. Personal data we process

  • Identity: first and last name, national ID / passport number, date of birth, nationality.
  • Contact: phone number, email address.
  • Stay and requests: check-in and check-out dates, room type, number of guests, special requests, event enquiries.
  • Customer transactions and finance: invoice and payment details.
  • Physical premises security: security camera footage.
  • Transaction security: access logs for the hotel internet network.
  • Contact form: the name, phone, dates and message you enter in the website form.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Handling reservations and providing accommodation, rooms and services.
  • Preparing event and hall quotes.
  • Responding to your requests and questions.
  • Issuing invoices and keeping financial records.
  • Meeting statutory notification obligations.
  • Ensuring the security of the premises, guests and staff.
  • Improving service quality.

4. Legal grounds

Your data is processed under Article 5/2 of KVKK: where it is directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract, necessary to comply with a legal obligation, necessary for the establishment, exercise or protection of a right, or necessary for our legitimate interests, provided your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed. Activities that need explicit consent, such as marketing communications, depend separately on your consent.

5. Transfers

Under Law No. 1774 on Identity Notification, and within the limits of KVKK Articles 8 and 9, your data may be shared with law enforcement, authorised public bodies and courts, with accountants, auditors and banks, and with business partners that provide software, hosting and communication services. When you use WhatsApp or Google services, your data may also be processed abroad by those providers.

6. How we collect data

We collect data verbally, in writing or electronically at the reception desk; by phone, email, WhatsApp and the form on our website; and through security cameras and network logs.

7. Your rights (KVKK Article 11)

You have the right to find out whether your data is processed; to request information about it; to learn its purpose and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; to request deletion or destruction under the conditions in the law; to request that these actions be notified to third parties; to object to a result against you arising from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

You can send your request in writing to the address above or to info@cityonekayseri.com. Requests are concluded within thirty days at the latest, depending on their nature. If you find our reply insufficient, you may file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board.

Privacy Policy

Information we collect on the site

What you enter in the contact and reservation form (name, phone, dates, guests, message) is not stored on a server by our website. When you press “Send”, it is passed to WhatsApp as a ready-made message and does not reach us until you send it. Once sent, your message is subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy terms.

Third-party services

Our site uses Google Fonts for typefaces and Google Maps for the location map. When you view the page, these services may receive technical information such as your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy for details.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. No transmission over the internet is completely risk-free.

Children’s privacy

Our site is not aimed at people under 18. Reservation requests must be made by adults.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page.

Cookie Policy

Cookies and similar technologies are small files stored in your browser when you visit a site.

What our site uses

  • Essential / functional: to remember your “Got it” choice, a c1-cookie entry is kept in your browser’s local storage. It contains no personal data and stays until you delete it.
  • Analytics and advertising: our site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Third party: Google Maps and Google Fonts may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block them, some features, such as the map or remembering your cookie choice, may not work.
